Momo, I'm really sorry. 🥺

I know sometimes I make mistakes, say things I shouldn't, or make things harder than they need to be. But one thing I never want you to doubt is how much I love you.

You are genuinely one of the most special people in my life. Your smile, your little habits, your way of talking, even the smallest things about you have become so precious to me.

I don't want a silly mistake or a bad moment to become bigger than what we have. I just want you to know that I'm sorry, I love you, and I care about you more than I sometimes know how to express.

I'm not asking you to forget everything instantly. I just hope you can forgive me when you're ready. ❤️

And if I had to choose again, after knowing everything, I'd still choose you.

I love you, Momo. ❤️
